# Better Every Day

This is the README for the Better Every Day website. Here you can stay up to date with our knowledge and tools as we continue to expand.

## What is Better Every Day?

Better Every Day is an offline first productivity system designed to help students and young professionals organize their work, capture their ideas, and make steady progress in whatever their discipline.

## Why use Better Every Day?

If you need a tracker or calendar to organize your work, see past and future tasks, or keep your notes together, Better Every Day brings those tools into one place. It helps you keep track of what you’ve done, plan what’s ahead, and stay focused on your progress.

Try us out at [BetterEveryDay.study](https://bettereveryday.study).

Our main application repository is [Better Every Day](https://github.com/ShaquilleCodrington/BetterEveryDay).
